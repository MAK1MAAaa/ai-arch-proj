# Hague cultural property supplement: source, processing and rights

The [dataset metadata](https://data.unesco.org/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/chp001) expressly assigns **CC BY-SA 4.0**, [license text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), attribution **UNESCO**. The normalized catalogue metadata, source-coordinate derivatives and their transformations are distributed under that same CC BY-SA 4.0 license. Attribution: **UNESCO, Cultural properties protected under the 1954 Hague Convention and its 1999 Second Protocol, UNESCO DataHub chp001; fixed snapshot and independently reviewed normalization by the ArchSearch project**. Processing includes identity/component crosswalking, selection of distinct fixed cultural places, field normalization, explicit exclusion of non-site storage locations from map data, and latitude/longitude order conversion. Earlier cohorts, Natural Earth, Cliopatria, Wikidata, software and other sources retain their separate licenses.

All175 source rows were audited:95 are admitted as distinct fixed cultural properties,35 correspond to existing entities,25 to existing components, and20 remain on hold. The source `whc` flag and spatial proximity are only diagnostic signals. They are not proof of a new identity, a protected-boundary containment test, political ownership or source-country inference. The new records are not all World Heritage inscriptions, and the source register is not a complete or geographically uniform global site inventory.

Original English/French names, source site IDs, UUIDs, source-country associations, protection metadata, public representative coordinates and component strings retain their source values. Per-property historical descriptions and original Chinese fields are absent and remain empty. No historical narrative, translated official Chinese description, construction year or continuous occupation period is synthesized. Registration/protection years are administrative source facts, not site chronology; all95 new historical-phase reviews remain `not_reviewed`.

The95 admitted properties have108 raw location-review entries.107 are eligible to map as the reviewed cultural places. The Arqa component explicitly titled Archaeological Warehouse and Storage (627) is retained in audit but excluded from cultural-site map/bbox points; it is not presented as an ancient excavation location. Menjez12 dolmens and two Mamluk source records preserve their source order and labels, including grouped names that do not imply extra coordinates. Main `coordinates` objects are longitude/latitude; component strings are latitude,longitude. No averaging, guessed centre, hidden-coordinate recovery or metre-level accuracy is asserted. Representatives remain separate from the source sequences, and source records do not become additional site counts.

UNESCO formal registration PDFs and original institutional pages were used to verify physical scope, including historical museum/library buildings. Their complete documents, photographs, maps and caches are not redistributed in Git. The DataHub license does not automatically relicense those auxiliary materials. Source references preserve actual URLs, byte hashes and known/unknown rights; links and factual scope notes do not constitute a new license claim. Independent project-agent checks are source/software review, not expert or human archaeology confirmation.

The distributed review binds fixed original-source and independent-review hashes and the unchanged1700-record base. Derived sites, source-location audit and manifest rebuild deterministically offline from those reviewed facts. Optional source-cache verification checks the cited fixed files without fetching or executing source documents. Multisource map metadata records each dataset's own version, license, source hash and site-identity group; it does not reattribute protection points to the World Heritage dataset.
