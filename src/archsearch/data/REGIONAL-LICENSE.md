# Regional cultural supplement: provenance and rights

Version: `regional-supplement-v1`, source evidence frozen and independently reviewed by agents on 2026-11-04. The review concerns identity, physical scope, cultural qualification, source geographic association, public representative coordinates and rights. It is not expert or human confirmation, and does not approve historical phases.

This cohort extends the unchanged 1663-record corpus in sparse northern, island and interior regions. These curated objects do not constitute a complete national, territorial or global inventory. Existing country codes identify source associations, not inferred historical sovereignty. Actual territory or area metadata, its original source and any local mapping to the existing region labels remain in the review provenance and public geographic notes.

Wikidata structured identifiers, labels, descriptions, aliases and selected statements are [CC0](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing). Selected coordinates retain the entity revision, statement GUID, rank, Earth globe and stated precision; a precision number is not a measured error or an excavation fix. Rejected statements are not silently corrected or substituted. For a site whose authority restricts location disclosure, no rejected numeric coordinate is republished in the distributed facts or maps; original source snapshots remain outside Git. Public snapshot hashes and identity references do not constitute release of restricted coordinates.

Nineteen Chinese descriptions are faithful project-agent translations of frozen CC0 Wikidata English short descriptions, independently compared with the original field by a second agent. They are explicitly identified as derived translations, not existing Wikidata Chinese fields or expert-approved history. The original English description is unchanged. `translation:wikidata:*` references retain the entity, field, revision and source hash; processing provenance additionally binds the translation table and independent review hashes. Records without a qualifying English description, authority-only records and an existing original Chinese description are not overwritten. No non-open authority prose is translated into a new narrative, and no unsupported event, use, date or coordinate is added.

Authority pages, museum records, heritage nominations and academic publications retain their individual rights. Each `source_refs` entry includes its publisher, origin, version/access date, excerpt and known license or unknown rights status. No blanket open license is applied to government-linked PDFs, national submissions, institutional websites, linked third-party material or images. Only factual metadata and short evidence excerpts are redistributed where no compatible reuse license is established. The compiler enforces a maximum combined distinct quotation of 25 words per source URI per record. Complete pages, complete publications, photographs, illustrations and original source caches are excluded from Git.

Shum Laka uses author-published site coordinates from the supplementary information of Lipson et al., Nature 2020, DOI [10.1038/s41586-020-1929-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-1929-1), archived by Harvard Medical School. The incomplete coordinate string on its official tentative submission is not used. Ishango has independent archaeological-site qualification from the Royal Belgian Institute of Natural Sciences; its IAU/ICOMOS original findspot is kept distinct from the Belgian museum display location and the former nearby village. No location of a movable museum object is presented as the archaeological original site.

Named cultural reserves, historic building complexes, fortifications, public archaeological places and monuments retain their stated scope. A broad reserve representative does not identify individual carvings. A reconstructed layout does not establish the original ancient position or a foundation year. World Heritage tentative status is not inscription, and national recognition is not automatically UNESCO recognition. All new historical phase states remain `not_reviewed`, with no default phase; sample ages, discovery dates, protection dates and nomination dates are not converted into continuous occupation, construction or use periods.

`regional_supplement_review.json` is the frozen fact and review specification. It binds source snapshots, selected coordinate origins, country/territory processing, independent review method and the complete earlier-record contract. Some archived evidence consists of canonical entity JSON or primary-source text snapshots rather than original HTTP response bytes; individual provenance preserves the method. `regional_sites.json` and `regional_manifest.json` are deterministic, independently pinned derivatives. They can be rebuilt offline from the distributed reviewed facts. Optional source-cache verification checks archived bytes only; it does not download, execute, relabel or reinterpret source documents. Prior cohorts and their separate source licenses remain unchanged.
