# Country cultural supplement: provenance and rights

Version: `country-supplement-v1`, accessed and independently source-reviewed by agents on 2026-11-04. This is an identity, cultural qualification, source country/region and coordinate review, not human/expert confirmation or approval of historical phases.

The 38 records add one cultural object for each of 37 missing country tags in the fixed World Heritage Convention source and Liechtenstein. Inclusion does not imply World Heritage inscription, formal protection, a sovereignty determination, a complete national inventory, or an exact excavation coordinate. The Nauru object is recorded as proposed heritage only. Three objects have no verified public digital coordinate; no substitute point is published.

Wikidata structured identifiers, labels, aliases, statements and selected representative coordinates are [CC0](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing). Statement GUID, Earth globe, rank, precision, entity revision and snapshot hash remain in the frozen review. Deprecated/ambiguous statements and invalid precision are not promoted into a location. Erroneous entity descriptions are excluded with the reason recorded. Cultural qualification additionally uses official heritage, archaeology, government, museum or memorial sources.

Authority sources retain their own rights. Every `source_refs` entry states its origin, version/access date and license or rights status. A link or metadata record does not license the linked page in full. Where no compatible reuse license is established, only factual metadata, attribution and short quoted evidence are included; the compiler limits their combined distinct excerpts to 25 words per source URI per record. No photographs, illustrations, complete source pages or complete publications are redistributed. In particular, the SPREP/Nauru record marked private is not relabelled as open data; only the factual object reference and seven-word identifying excerpt are retained. Cook Islands Arai Te Tonga archaeology coordinates derive from the POFATU database under its stated CC BY 4.0 license, together with a separate official cultural identity reference.

`country_supplement_review.json` contains the frozen reviewed fact specification, country association evidence, selected coordinate origin, individual rights, independent agent review method and 153 distinct archived source-file SHA256 contracts. Some entity files are canonical `wbgetentities` JSON snapshots and some authority evidence uses primary-source text snapshots; they are not all original HTTP response bytes. Raw page/PDF/entity caches are excluded from Git. `supplement_sites.json` and `supplement_manifest.json` are deterministic derivatives, with unchanged base-record hashes and independent fixed payload hashes. The review fact specification can be compiled offline without those caches. Optional cache verification checks bytes against the archived contracts and does not fetch, execute or reinterpret source documents.

All 38 historical phase statuses remain `not_reviewed`; no foundation year, continuous lifecycle, construction event or political affiliation is inferred. Country/region metadata is a source association. The Liechtenstein Europe assignment is explicitly a processed M49 region mapping, not a claim of World Heritage Convention membership. Existing data cohorts and their separate licenses remain unchanged.
