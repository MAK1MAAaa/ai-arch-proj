# South America cultural supplement: provenance and rights

Version: `southamerica-supplement-v1`. Source facts were frozen and independently compared by project agents on 2026-11-04. The 11 records extend the unchanged 1689-record corpus with six existing source-country associations: Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Peru and Uruguay. This is a curated cultural sample, not a complete South American, national or archaeological inventory. Modern source associations and a mapped representative do not establish historical sovereignty.

Wikidata identifiers, original labels, aliases, short descriptions, country associations and selected coordinate statements are [CC0](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing). Selected Wikidata positions retain their entity revision, statement GUID, active rank, Earth globe and stated precision. Precision is source metadata, not a measured error, proof that a point is inside a protected boundary or an excavation coordinate. Missing precision stays missing; rejected positions are not averaged, repaired or substituted with nearby towns, museum locations or natural-feature centres.

Chinese descriptions are faithful project-agent translations of frozen CC0 Wikidata English short descriptions, independently compared with the original field by a separate agent. The original English descriptions remain unchanged. `translation:wikidata:*` references identify derived translations, rather than original Wikidata Chinese fields or expert-approved history. Provenance binds each original field, entity revision and source SHA256 to the translation table and independent review hashes. No government or non-open authority narrative is translated into a new historical account, and no additional event, use, date or coordinate is asserted.

National heritage authorities, official tentative submissions, institutional websites and publications retain their individual rights. `source_refs` records publisher, source URI, version/access date, short excerpt and known license or an explicitly unknown license. Only factual metadata and short source evidence are distributed when no compatible reuse license has been established; the compiler caps combined distinct quotations from such a source at 25 words per URI per record. Full pages, publications, original caches, photographs, maps and other media are excluded from Git. Government hosting or UNESCO linking does not create a blanket reuse license. A tentative nomination is not World Heritage inscription.

Lapa do Santo uses the whole-shelter coordinate published by Strauss et al. in the author article [PLOS ONE, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0137456](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0137456), licensed CC BY 4.0. The explicit author DMS is converted without averaging a different Wikidata point or substituting an individual burial position; no unstated datum or physical accuracy is invented. Incallajta uses the 2006 official nomination-table point for entry 1218 in [WHC-06/30.COM/8B](https://whc.unesco.org/archive/2006/whc06-30com-08Be.pdf), explicitly qualified as an approximate centre. This coordinate source is kept separate from the cultural qualification source and does not assert an inscribed status or a verified boundary.

Chamangá is represented as one rock-art locality, using the public point of the same Wikidata entity. It is not a collection of separately geolocated painting-rock records, and the point is not certified to be inside an official protected boundary. The source map's YACARE datum is not silently treated as WGS84 or transformed without a supported basis. Santa Elina retains its independently supported cultural identity with `repr_point=null`: no trustworthy public numeric point was selected from the reviewed evidence. This is not a claim that its real location is unknown or officially restricted. The invalid 93-second longitude in the published text is not silently carried or corrected; a conflicting Wikidata point is not adopted. Null never becomes zero or a nearby regional centre.

Independent review covers source identity, physical scope, cultural qualification, public representative positioning, source geographic association, rights and existing-record deduplication. It is not expert or human archaeology confirmation and approves no historical phases. All new chronology states remain `not_reviewed`, with no phases or default phase; discovery, protection, publication and nomination dates are not converted into construction, continuous occupation or use periods.

`southamerica_review.json` preserves frozen facts, individual review decisions, source artifact hashes and original statement evidence. It binds the original candidate and independent source-review reports, the separate translation review and the complete earlier-record contract. Some source artifacts are canonical entity JSON or explicitly recorded primary-page text extracts, not raw HTTP bytes; the exact archive method remains in provenance. `southamerica_sites.json` and `southamerica_manifest.json` are deterministic pinned derivatives that rebuild offline from distributed facts. Optional cache verification checks the 38 archived evidence files without downloading, executing or reinterpreting them. Prior cohorts, source-point sequences and their separate licenses remain unchanged.
