# Antarctic Treaty Secretariat HSM data attribution

The Antarctic Historic Sites and Monuments records in `polar_sites.json` derive from the
[Antarctic Treaty Secretariat APA database](https://www.ats.aq/devph/en/apa-database),
retrieved on 2026-11-04. Copyright Antarctic Treaty Secretariat.

The Secretariat's [copyright statement](https://www.ats.aq/e/copyright.html) identifies
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The source, query and individual record hashes are frozen in `polar_manifest.json`.

Processing includes HTML-to-text and whitespace normalization, source-number namespacing,
separation of designation metadata from historical phases, and an explicit DMS conversion for
HSM 34. HSM 95 has no verified wreck location: its last-sighting reference remains explanatory
text and is not a site marker. Original polar latitudes remain unchanged. Proposing and managing
Parties are not treated as sovereign owners of Antarctic sites.

Photographs, videos and other media are excluded. This processing does not imply endorsement
by the Antarctic Treaty Secretariat.
